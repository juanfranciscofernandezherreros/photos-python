# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and empathetic.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, never on people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when necessary, and learn from mistakes.
- Protect other participants' privacy and safety.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository maintainers or GitHub's reporting tools. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and whenever someone publicly represents the project.
